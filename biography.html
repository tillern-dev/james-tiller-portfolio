Biography

James Tiller Munyerere, also known as James Tillern, is a Kenyan technology enthusiast, university student and aspiring software engineer. He is the creator of StudyTrack, a student productivity application, and the founder of TILLERN LABS, an emerging technology brand focused on practical digital solutions.

Alongside his university studies and interest in technology, James also works as a barber at Barber & Beyond Hair Studio.

Early interest in technology

James developed an interest in computers and technology during primary school. His curiosity about computers grew into a lasting interest in programming, software systems and digital innovation. This early enthusiasm helped shape his ambition to pursue software engineering.

University education

James is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Tourism Management at the University of Eldoret, Kenya. While studying tourism, he independently explores programming, mobile application development and backend technologies. He believes that academic studies and personal interests can complement one another.

Professional experience

James also works as a barber at Barber & Beyond Hair Studio, developing customer service, communication, creativity and attention to detail. He balances his work with university studies and continued learning in software development.

Software development journey

Through self-directed learning, experimentation and practical problem-solving, James has been developing his technical skills. He has explored web technologies, mobile application development and backend programming, learning from the process of building and debugging software.

Featured project

StudyTrack

StudyTrack is an Android application created by James to help students organize assignments, lecture timetables, academic courses and examination schedules. It represents an important milestone in his journey from learning about software to developing a functioning application. He continues to improve StudyTrack through ongoing updates.

TILLERN LABS

James founded TILLERN LABS as an emerging technology brand through which he explores software development and digital innovation. His goal is to continue building useful solutions as his experience and skills grow.

Future aspirations

James aspires to become a professional software engineer, with a particular interest in backend development and reliable digital systems. He is committed to continuous learning and hopes to apply technology to real-world challenges.
